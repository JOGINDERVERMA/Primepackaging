Packaging that Performs
When It Matters Most

At Prime Packaging, we believe that great packaging is more than just a bag—it's the first line of protection for your products and an important part of your brand's reputation.

Based in Sardarshahar, Churu, Rajasthan, Prime Packaging is a trusted manufacturer of PP Woven Bags, HDPE Woven Bags, BOPP Laminated Bags, Food Grade Woven Bags, Leno Mesh Bags, Jumbo Bags (FIBC), and Custom Printed Packaging Solutions. We proudly serve businesses across Rajasthan and India with durable, reliable, and customized woven packaging designed for a wide range of industries.

Our Story

Every business depends on packaging that protects its products from production to delivery. A weak or poorly manufactured bag can result in damaged goods, transportation losses, and unhappy customers.

Prime Packaging was established with a simple objective—to manufacture woven bags that businesses can trust. Over the years, we have invested in modern manufacturing technology, premium raw materials, and skilled professionals to build a production system that prioritizes quality at every stage.

Our Manufacturing Process

From selecting premium-grade PP and HDPE raw materials to final inspection, every step is carefully monitored by experienced professionals.

  • Extrusion & Weaving

    Controlled precision weaving on high-speed circular looms ensures structural integrity.

  • Lamination & Printing

    Rotogravure and flexo printing processes for excellent brand clarity and vibrant colors.

  • Testing & Dispatch

    Each batch undergoes multiple quality checks in our laboratory before being packed.
